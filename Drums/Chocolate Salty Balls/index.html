Chef from South Park - "Chocolate Salty Balls (P.S. I Love You)"

This funky innuendo-filled number, sung by soul singer Issac Hayes as the South Park character Chef, was very nearly the UK's Christmas no. 1 in 1998, but was unfortunately held off by the Spice Girls. For a comedy song, it is surprisingly difficult to play!

In case you're wondering, the insanely difficult final section, which I've tried to make as accurate as possible to the recording, I personally never actually play. I tend to suppliment it with a "Teen Spririt"-esque groove, while keeping the funk intact. I'd advise you do similar.

Also yes, I know the first repeat is broken in the MIDI and MP3 files. If you can find a way to fix it, be my guest.
