Beastie Boys - "Intergalactic"

One of the Beastie Boy's most famous songs, the constant punctuation of their lines creates regular fills which really need sheet music to keep track of!

This song was made using samples and a drum machine, and as such the tone of the drums change at points in the song. These points have been noted in the sheet music.
