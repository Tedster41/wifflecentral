The Beatles - "Octopus's Garden"

One of the two Beatles songs written by drummer Ringo Starr, "Octopus's Garden" has become one of the band's most famous songs, thanks in part to its childish appeal and place on the band's most iconic record, Abbey Road. The drum part is pretty simple, and should be played with a hint of swing.
