B-Side Guide

Take That - Beautiful World

While Take That had disbanded in 1996, they reunited in 2005 for a documentary about their experiences, alongside a tie-in greatest hits album, containing one new song, recorded by the reunited band. The success of the album, which is now the 60th best-selling album in British history, led to the band embarking on a tour to capitalise on this. After the tour was completed in 2006, the group decided that they wanted to record a new album, and so they did. Beautiful World, their fourth studio album overall, was released in November 2006, and it contains the most bonus tracks and B-sides of any Take That album, so it's no surprise it's appeared here. The first, which isn't really a bonus track, is "Butterfly", which plays after a minute of silence in track 11. So, yes, it's a hidden track. That's not a bonus track, that's just a song on the album. Despite this, Take That's record label Polydor had the audacity to list it on the back as a "bonus track" of some copies, even though it was present on all of them (and still in hidden form!). Because the bonus tracks will be put on the end of the album here, this will be extracted from track 11 and placed as track 12.

Now, to start the actual bonus tracks, we have everyone's favourite, the Japanese bonus track! "6 in the Morning Fool" takes this role. Next we have "Beautiful Morning", the B-side to the lead single "Patience", which would also appear on the "Tour edition" of the album, released (as you might expect) at booths along the tour trail of the ensuing tour for this album. "Trouble With Me" was released as a B-side in Mainland Europe, as well as on an iTunes exclusive version of the single. The next B-sides come from the second single, "Shine", with the main one being "Trouble With Me" from earlier, but also some versions containing a "Stripped Down" version of "Patience", as well as the track "We Love to Entertain You" on other versions. There are two third singles from the album, one released in the UK, and one released in the rest of Europe. They both contained the B-side "We All Fall Down", however the UK release contained the original, which also appeared on the Tour edition, while the European version contained an acoustic version of a track. I am a little unsure on whether to include acoustic versions here (mainly because they're so generic) but since there isn't enough room on the disc for all of the B-sides, this one will have to be dropped for space, but the original version will stay. And finally, the fourth single released from the album kind of wasn't. "Rule the World", funnily enough the song you'll most likely recognise from this album, isn't actually on it. It was written for the film Stardust after the album was released, but because the band at the time were about to start touring for the Beautiful World album, the track was given a cover in the style of the rest of the singles from the album and placed on the Deluxe tour edition of the album, important as the track was in the tour's setlist. This effectively makes it a single from the album, although it isn't really on it. And so, it, and its B-side "Stay Together", are included in our bonus tracks. One thing of note also is that the tour edition of the album included a version of the track a full minute longer than the radio edit, which is the version we'll be using (generally because I like things in their full length).
