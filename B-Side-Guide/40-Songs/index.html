Jay Foreman - 40 Songs

Jay Foreman, who you'll likely know today for being behind the Map Men series, started off at York University making music. To begin with, he had three separate albums: Raisins Turn Into Grapes in the Microwave (2005), You Are NOT Superdog! (2006) and Flush, You Bastard! (2007). However in 2007, he merged these albums into one album, titled 20 Songs. A few songs from the original three albums didn't make the jump, but most made it across. 20 Songs would receive updates in 2011, have a "For Kids" edition in 2013, before the final version, which is still for sale today, would be created. Through these updates, old songs have been removed and new songs have been added, and so a number of these songs are now lost media. But assuming they aren't, I decided to assemble all the songs which have been featured in the 20 Songs series into one album, miraculously creating a tracklist with exactly 40 songs.

Now one thing that should be noted about this tracklist is the fact that I do not know the durations for any of them - like I said, a number of these songs are lost media, and so I couldn't effectively judge if this would all fit on one disc. Given its forty tracks, probably not. But in science everything must be proven with facts, so until then let's just quietly assume they all fit on one disc.
