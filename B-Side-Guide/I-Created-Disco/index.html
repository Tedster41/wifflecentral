Calvin Harris - I Created Disco

Calvin Harris' debut album, an assortment of Amiga noises arranged into an hour of music, did not paint a bright future for the now legendary DJ. Following this he would stop making music on his Amiga and started making some much better received and much better known songs. However, just to torture those reviewers who said that this album is bad (I personally quite like it), I've assembled all of the B-sides and bonus tracks for this album too.

First, we have an iTunes Store bonus track, pretty standard, and then some B-sides from the three singles from the album, "Acceptable in the 80s", "The Girls" and "Merrymaking at My Place". The last two are a bit more peculiar. The first is "Stillness in Time", a cover of a Jamiroquai song, which appeared on the compilation album Radio 1 Established 1967, which featured covers of classic songs by then-contemporary artists. The album, released in late 2007, featured Amiga-era Calvin Harris, and he totally transforms the original into one of his little sonic experiments. The last track is "1984", a song released on one of his early sampler/demo tapes, simply titled Calvin Harris. This song never saw an official release aside from this, but I felt it was worth including.

But since this is Harris' debut album, it also means we get to dig around in his early stuff before the album. Well, under the name "Stouffer", he released "Da Bongos" and "Brighter Days" in 2002. Then, under the Calvin Harris name, he released "Let Me Know" and "Hip Hop" in 2004. Another early cut, "Rock Band", was released in 2006. As for early versions of the tracks on the album, early promos and samplers give us the demo versions of "Acceptable in the 80s", "The Girls", and "This is the Industry". Also present is an unmastered extended version of "The Girls". Together, these form this tracklist:
