Man With No Name - Earth Moving the Sun

Man With No Name's second album takes a more commercial angle while sticking to his roots in Goa trance. There isn't too much to cover here, so we'll get through it quickly. Firstly, despite finding major success as an albums and singles artist, the man still felt the need to make two exclusive tracks for compilations, maybe just for old times sake or something. These were "Retox" for Kiss the Future and "Equatoria" for Electrosect. The only other part of his discography worth mentioning is the Japanese release of this album, which introduces two new songs ("Mainframe" and "Under the Influence"), and also includes instrumental versions of the two vocal songs from the album. While I usually skip over instrumentals here, the criticism of inserting words into songs which work fine without them for commercial appeal is one of the most regular things found in the comments section of trance releases on Discogs (e.g. "This promo single uses the instrumental version - BUY THIS ONE!!!"), so for the sake of that lot I'll put the instrumentals on as well. And to be fair, at least regarding the vocal tracks on this album, they do have a point.
