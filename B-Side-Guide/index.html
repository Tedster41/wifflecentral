Ted's B-Side Guide

Hello! If you've navigated your way here, which you seem to have, welcome! This is a special part of my website for me to show you something I work on in my spare time. That being finding all of the bonus tracks and little titbits that didn't make it to an album, and instead appeared as a B-side or possibly an outtake that would be released years later. The main use of this page is to show you, yes, you, the bonus tracks, the B-sides and the outtakes from your my favourite albums, and how they can be arranged into a neat little tracklist alongside them. Crucially, this does not discuss external remixes (remixes made by artists other than the song's original creator) because the line between official and unofficial remixes is very blurred. Live versions (including when they are used as B-sides) are also excluded for similar reasons; the amount of live bootlegs out there makes it pointless to try and collect all the live versions of a certain song (although Peel sessions are often excused for electronic artists when they feature remixed versions of existing songs). This page also has a couple album concepts (e.g. vinyl editions and compilations).
