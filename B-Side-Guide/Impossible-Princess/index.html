Kylie Minogue - Impossible Princess

Only a quick one here, but I felt it was worth passing by because of the unusual way these bonus tracks were released. Kylie's Impossible Princess was her most introspective album, and jumps through a number of styles: trip hop, trance, and a bit of Blur-style Britpop. Sort of like a watered down Björk, and that's no insult - anything which sounds like Björk is on to something. Being a pop record, the B-sides are a bit thin: there's only two of them, "Love Takes Over Me" and "Tears". But things take a bit of a turn in 2000. Impossible Princess and its predecessor, Kylie Minogue, were released on the British Deconstruction label, co-run by Mike Pickering of M People. Fed up with the handling of the album, which was delayed several times by the label (in the UK, it was issued over a year after its originally planned release date), Kylie left the label in late 1997 - before the album was released in the UK - and released her next studio album, Light Years (2000), on Parlophone. Shortly after this, Deconstruction, still owning the rights to the music for the previous two albums, took advantage of all the Kylie promotion at the time to release a compilation, titled Hits+. The idea being that, since they didn't own nearly enough of Kylie's catalogue to issue a fully-fledged greatest hits album (she had done four albums before signing to Deconstruction), they would issue a compilation of the singles they had the rights to (the Hits portion), and then a collection of previously unreleased outtakes (the + portion). And so, an additional three unreleased tracks from the Impossible Princess sessions were released on this compilation, taking our total to five. Not bad, and just short enough to fit on the CD with the album.

A few footnotes: "Take Me with You" was released in 1998 on the promo Other Sides, which was exclusive to Australian copies of the album sold in HMV. Considering this wasn't a worldwide release (and there were only roughly 28 HMV shops in Australia at the time), I'm still classing its first proper release as being on Hits+. On the other side of the coin, "This Girl" is only present on Australian copies of the Hits+ album. And as for remixes? The album's primary producers, Brothers in Rhythm, made two remixes of "Too Much", and none of the other producers who worked on the album did any remixes for the project.
