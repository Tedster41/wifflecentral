B-Side Guide

Take That - Take That & Party

Take That's first album, Take That & Party, is an utter cheese fest, which only true cheese connoisseurs can stand. And so, for those of us who can get through it, I've added the B-sides from the various singles (yes, all seven of them) that came from this album on the end of the actual thing as an additional challenge. Some album tracks were used as B-sides on singles released before it, but there were non-album B-sides on singles from both before and after the album's release. There are five B-sides, two from singles and three from The Love Songs EP, which was technically the single release of A-side "A Million Love Songs", it's just they felt like putting three extra songs on the single and calling it an EP. Also included is the original version of album track "Never Want to Let You Go", which was remixed for the album after appearing as a B-side of "It Only Takes a Minute", and the single remix of "Could It Be Magic", the album's seventh and final single.

Now, if you're a true Take That fan, you'll probably think of the 'Expanded' version of the album, but that only contains two bonus B-sides and an external remix. We're doing much better than that here.
