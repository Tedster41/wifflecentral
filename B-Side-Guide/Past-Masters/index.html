Paul McCartney & Wings - Past Masters

One of the many (many) revolutionary things about the Beatles was how they structured their releases. 14-track albums with singles which (largely) weren't included on albums. Most bands then and now would always put their hit single on their new album to help promote it, but this is the fudging Beatles we're talking about. They didn't need any promotion. Now, when the four went their separate ways, we got to see who's idea this whole thing was: John and Paul's. Ringo had a few non-album singles, as did George, but John and Paul were constantly putting them out. Paul in particular would do it the Beatles-style of taking the best song from an album's sessions and releasing that as a single in promotion of the album, but then don't put the single on it (e.g. "Another Day" and Ram). John would just put out a non-album single without an album because he felt like it (e.g. "Cold Turkey"). In 1987, when the Beatles' Apple Records was standardising the band's catalogue, they settled on creating a new compilation titled Past Masters to hold all of the band's non-album stuff, so they could release all of the band's catalogue in a box set. Now, creating a Past Masters for all of Paul's solo career would take several CDs, considering how long his solo career is, but, of course, Paul technically didn't have much of a solo career in the 70s because he was a member of Wings, a new band that he founded. Wings lasted from 1971 to 1981, releasing seven albums in that time, as well as a number of singles. So rather than compiling the non-album stuff for each album, I felt it would be much more in-character for Paul to have a big compilation with all of them!

Some quick rules before we get into it: Paul's actual solo stuff from the 70s ("Another Day", "Wonderful Christmastime") won't be included on the album. Nor will the Wings releases credited to other names, so "Walking in the Park with Eloise" (by the Country Hams) and "Seaside Woman" (by Suzy and the Red Stripes), or even Wings songs released as B-sides after the band had broken up ("My Carnival" and "Mama's Little Girl"). Why? Because they won't fit. Sorry.
